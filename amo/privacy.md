# Privacy policy

Ultra Downloader's Firefox extension does not collect, store remotely or
transmit any personal data.

## What is processed

- The URL of a download you click or start in Firefox.
- The current page path and the clicked link's label, only to decide whether a
  click is a download control.
- The URL/port of the local Ultra Downloader app (`127.0.0.1`).

## Where it goes

Download URLs are sent only to the Ultra Downloader desktop app running on the
same computer over a loopback connection. They are not sent to GitHub, Mozilla
or any other server by the extension.

## What is not collected

- No browsing history
- No page content
- No cookies
- No analytics or telemetry
- No accounts or identifiers

## Storage

The extension does not store download history or settings. The desktop app
stores its own download queue on your computer.
